I am a philosopher working on the philosophy of language and mind. I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Chair for Ethics at the University of Zurich. From 2027, I will lead the SNSF Ambizione project Cognitive Chimeras: The Dynamics of Category Extension in the Hierarchy of Concepts at the University of Neuchâtel. I also serve as a section editor of the journal Experimental Philosophy.

My research asks how we categorize and evaluate with words: how thick and thin evaluative concepts work, what dual character concepts are, and how categories are extended and change over time. I combine philosophical analysis with experimental philosophy, corpus linguistics, and computational methods from natural language processing, including large language models.
